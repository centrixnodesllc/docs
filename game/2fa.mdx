## Step 1: Open the Game Panel

Head to the [game panel](https://panel.centrixnodes.net/).

<img
  src="/images/game/2fa/gamelanding.jpg"
  alt="Game Panel Landing Page"
/>


## Step 2: Open the Account Section

Log in to the game panel and click **Account** in the bottom left corner of the page.

<img
  src="/images/game/2fa/2falanding.jpg"
  alt="Game Panel Account Section"
/>

## Step 3: Enable 2FA

Click the **Enable 2FA** button to start the setup process.

<img
  src="/images/game/2fa/2fasetup.jpg"
  alt="Enable 2FA Button"
/>

## Step 4: Configure Your Authenticator App

Follow the instructions to set up 2FA using an authenticator app (e.g., Google Authenticator, Authy). Scan the QR code or enter the provided key into your authenticator app.


<Check>
  **Congratulations!** If you've followed all the steps correctly, you now have 2FA enabled on your account. This adds an extra layer of security to your game panel account and helps protect your server and data from unauthorized access. Keep your authenticator app secure and do not share your 2FA codes with anyone.
</Check>
